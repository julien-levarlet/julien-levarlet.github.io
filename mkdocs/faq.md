# FAQ

## Why am I hearing the same bird again?

In Training, birds are repeated according to spaced repetition. Incorrect answers return sooner, while consistently correct birds appear less often. In a long or small quiz pool, repeats are expected. Challenge uses a random order instead.

## Does difficulty change which birds appear?

Mostly, it changes how similar the wrong answers are to the target. The observation-frequency range also filters the General Quiz. Other quiz types have their own selection rules; see [How birds are selected](bird-selection.md).

## How does a bird become Mastered?

Each species has a learning score from 0 to 3. A correct answer on the first try raises it by one; a completed question with a mistake lowers it by one. A score of 0 is **Review**, 1 or 2 is **Learning**, and 3 is **Mastered**. See [Learning and scoring](learning-and-scoring.md) for details about retries and Training review.

## How are Challenge points calculated?

Finite Challenge quizzes give one point for each correct answer. The endless General Quiz normally gives more points when the three alternatives are closer taxonomic matches. Expert mode makes every correct answer worth one point. The [Learning and scoring page](learning-and-scoring.md) includes the calculation.

## What does Expert mode do?

Expert mode changes scoring, not the bird pool or the similarity of the answer choices. In Challenge it fixes each correct answer at one point. To make distractors more closely related, change **Answer similarity** in the difficulty options.

## Why can I not start a quiz or see a quiz level?

The current area, season, list, or learning group may not contain enough birds. A multiple-choice question needs four species. Choose a broader area or season, another quiz type, or add birds to your list. My Lists requires at least four birds.

## Why is a recording missing or not playing?

Recordings are retrieved from Xeno-canto and need an internet connection. Check your connection and device media volume, then retry the recording. The source may have no suitable recording available or may be temporarily unavailable.

## Why is a bird image or Wikipedia summary missing?

Bird information is retrieved online and is not available for every species or language. Check your connection, try another bird language in Settings, or open the bird's source link if one is shown.

## What happens when I get an answer wrong?

In Training, you can try again and the image may reveal more depending on your image setting. In Challenge, the wrong answer uses one of your three lives. The missed bird also receives more review in Training.

## Is my progress saved?

Yes. Quiz scores and completion status are saved for Challenge. Learning status and Training repetition progress are also stored on your device. Erasing app data removes local progress and settings.

## Where can I report a problem?

ChirpQuiz is still under development, so please send feedback and bug reports to [birdquiz.contact@gmail.com](mailto:birdquiz.contact@gmail.com). Include the quiz type, area and season, and what happened; avoid including personal information you do not need to share. You can also find developer information under **Settings > Credits** in the app.
