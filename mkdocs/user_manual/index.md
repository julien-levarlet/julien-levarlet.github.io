# ChirpQuiz User Guide

ChirpQuiz helps you learn bird sounds through short listening quizzes. Choose a quiz, listen to a recording, and identify the species from four choices. You can practice without score pressure or take on a challenge with three lives.

This guide explains how the quizzes choose birds and answers, what the difficulty settings change, and where the app's bird information and recordings come from.

## Help improve ChirpQuiz

ChirpQuiz is still under development, and your feedback can directly help make it better. Please contact me to report bugs, point out confusing parts, request features, or share ideas about how the quizzes and bird information could improve.

**Email the developer:** [birdquiz.contact@gmail.com](mailto:birdquiz.contact@gmail.com)

You can also open **Settings > Credits** in the app to find the developer information. The app may also invite you to send feedback after you have learned a few birds.

## A quick note about quiz settings

The selected area and season affect which birds are available in area-based quizzes. The difficulty setting mainly affects how similar the three incorrect answers are to the correct bird. These are separate controls; see [How birds are selected](../bird-selection.md) and [Difficulty](../difficulty.md) for details.
