# Bird data, recordings, and images

I bring together bird information from a few independent sources: **GBIF** for bird data and distribution information, **Xeno-canto** for recordings, and **Wikipedia** and **Wikimedia Commons** for summaries and images. These projects provide the content, I don't own or create it.

## Sources

- [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/): bird data, including distribution information.
- [Xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org/): bird recordings. You can contribute recordings on [Xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org/about/xeno-canto).
- [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/) and [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/): bird summaries and images. You can contribute to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Community_portal) and Wikimedia Commons.
