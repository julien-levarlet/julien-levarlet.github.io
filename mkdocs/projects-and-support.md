# Xeno-canto and Wikipedia

ChirpQuiz brings together bird data from several sources. In particular, it uses **Xeno-canto** for bird recordings and **Wikipedia** for short summaries and many bird images. These are independent community projects; ChirpQuiz does not own or create their content.

## Xeno-canto

[Xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org/) is a community collection of wildlife sound recordings, including bird calls and songs. ChirpQuiz looks up recordings for a species and uses one in a quiz. A bird's detail page can show several recordings and identify their recordists.

You can support Xeno-canto by contributing your own well-identified recordings, adding accurate species and recording details, and following the project's recording and upload guidance on its [contribute page](https://xeno-canto.org/contribute). Good metadata helps listeners and researchers find and use recordings.

Recordings are contributed by many people, so availability and quality can vary. Visit the recording's Xeno-canto page for its recordist, details, and any applicable conditions for reuse.

## Wikipedia and Wikimedia

[Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/) is a collaboratively written, free encyclopedia. ChirpQuiz looks up a short introduction to a bird and may use a page image. Selecting a source link opens the relevant Wikipedia page or image information when available.

You can help by correcting errors, adding reliable references, improving bird articles, or translating useful information. Start with Wikipedia's [community portal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Community_portal) and follow its guidance on sourcing and editing. Images may come from Wikipedia or Wikimedia Commons; check the individual image page for its creator and reuse terms.

If you would like to support Wikimedia financially, see the [Wikimedia Foundation donation page](https://donate.wikimedia.org/). Donations support the nonprofit that operates Wikipedia and other Wikimedia projects.
